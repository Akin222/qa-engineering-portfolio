# QA Risk Assessment

## Purpose

Risk assessment helps prioritize testing effort toward functionality where failures could have the greatest impact on users, business operations, or product quality.

## Risk Factors

Risk can be considered using:

- User impact
- Business impact
- Functional complexity
- Frequency of use
- Integration dependencies
- Failure consequences
- Likelihood of failure

## High-Risk Areas

Typical high-risk areas for an e-commerce application include:

- Product discovery
- Search
- Product interaction
- Shopping cart
- Checkout-related flows
- Authentication
- Payment-related functionality
- Critical integrations

## Testing Strategy

Higher-risk functionality should receive deeper functional, exploratory, negative, regression, and end-to-end testing where applicable.

Risk assessment should be revisited when significant application changes are introduced.
