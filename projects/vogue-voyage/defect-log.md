# Defect Log

## Purpose

The defect log provides a centralized view of issues identified during testing and their current status.

## Status Definitions

- New — Defect has been identified and documented.
- In Progress — Investigation or development work is underway.
- Fixed — A fix has been provided for verification.
- Retest — QA verification is in progress.
- Reopened — The defect remains reproducible after the reported fix.
- Closed — The defect has been successfully verified and closed.

## Notes

The log should be updated as defects progress through their lifecycle.
