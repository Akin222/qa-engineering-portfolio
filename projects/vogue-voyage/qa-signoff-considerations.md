# QA Sign-Off Considerations

## Purpose

QA sign-off should communicate the testing evidence and known quality risks available at the time of the assessment.

## Sign-Off Information

A QA assessment should consider:

- Testing scope
- Test execution status
- Critical user journeys
- Defect status
- Regression results
- Outstanding risks
- Known limitations
- Unresolved high-impact issues

## Quality Assessment

QA sign-off does not mean that software is guaranteed to be defect-free.

It communicates whether the available testing evidence supports the intended release decision within the agreed scope.

## Outstanding Risks

Any unresolved issue that could materially affect users, business operations, security, reliability, or critical functionality should be clearly communicated.

## Final Principle

Quality decisions should be based on evidence, risk, and transparency rather than simply reporting a pass percentage.
