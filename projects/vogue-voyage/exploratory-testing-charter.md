# Exploratory Testing Charter

## Objective

Explore the application beyond predefined scenarios to identify unexpected behavior, usability problems, inconsistent states, and defects that may not be captured by scripted testing.

## Exploration Areas

- Navigation
- Search
- Product interactions
- Shopping cart
- Unexpected inputs
- Rapid user interactions
- Empty states
- Error states
- Responsive behavior

## Approach

Exploration is guided by risk, user behavior, previous observations, and areas where application behavior appears inconsistent.

Relevant findings should be documented with sufficient evidence to support investigation and reproduction.
