## Environment Validation

Before testing, verify that:

- The application is accessible.
- Required accounts are available.
- Test wallets are configured correctly.
- Required test assets are available.
- The selected blockchain network matches the intended environment.
- Required APIs or integrations are reachable.

## QA Principle

A known and documented environment improves reproducibility and helps distinguish application defects from environment-specific problems.
