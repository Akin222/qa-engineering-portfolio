# QBitFlow Responsive Testing

## Objective

Validate that the payment experience remains usable across relevant viewport sizes and devices.

## Areas

- Checkout layout
- Payment instructions
- Buttons
- Form controls
- Status information
- Error messages
- Dashboard layout
- Horizontal scrolling
- Content visibility

## Viewport Categories

- Desktop
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Mobile

## Validation

Verify that important payment actions remain visible, accessible, and usable without unintended layout problems.

## Risk

Responsive defects in payment flows can prevent users from completing transactions even when the underlying payment functionality works correctly.
