# QBitFlow User Journey Validation

## Customer Journey

1. Open payment experience.
2. Review payment information.
3. Select or use the supported payment configuration.
4. Submit payment.
5. Observe payment status.
6. Wait for confirmation.
7. Verify final outcome.

## Merchant Journey

1. Open merchant dashboard.
2. Locate payment activity.
3. Review transaction information.
4. Verify payment status.
5. Verify settlement information.

## QA Focus

The customer and merchant experiences should remain logically consistent throughout the transaction lifecycle.

## Risk

A successful customer-facing state combined with an inconsistent merchant or settlement state represents a significant quality risk.
