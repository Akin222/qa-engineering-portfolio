# QBitFlow Test Completion Criteria

## Objective

Define the conditions used to determine whether the planned QA scope has been sufficiently evaluated.

## Completion Considerations

Testing may be considered complete when:

- Critical planned scenarios have been executed.
- High-risk business rules have been evaluated.
- Blocking defects have been addressed or formally accepted.
- Important failed scenarios have been documented.
- Required regression testing has been performed.
- Test evidence has been collected.
- Known limitations have been documented.

## Important Note

Completion does not mean that the product is guaranteed to contain no defects.

It means that the defined testing scope has reached its agreed completion criteria and remaining risks are understood.

## QA Principle

A clear completion criterion prevents testing from ending simply because the available time has expired.
