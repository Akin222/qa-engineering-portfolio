# QBitFlow Exploratory Testing Charter

## Mission

Explore the payment workflow to identify unexpected behavior that may not be covered by predefined test scenarios.

## Areas of Exploration

- Checkout navigation
- Payment instructions
- Network selection
- Asset selection
- Payment status changes
- Error handling
- Expiry behavior
- Dashboard synchronization
- Recovery after interruption

## Questions

- Can a user become confused about payment status?
- Can the UI display information inconsistent with the transaction state?
- What happens when actions are repeated?
- What happens after a delayed response?
- Are unexpected states communicated clearly?

## Evidence

Exploratory findings should be supported with reproducible steps and appropriate evidence.

## QA Principle

Exploratory testing complements scripted testing by allowing the tester to investigate risks revealed during execution.
