# QBitFlow QA Strategy Summary

## Testing Philosophy

The QBitFlow QA approach combines functional, exploratory, negative, integration, API, regression, usability, accessibility, and risk-based testing.

## Primary Focus

The highest priority is the complete payment journey:

Customer Checkout
? Payment
? Confirmation
? Settlement
? Merchant Outcome

## Risk-Based Approach

Testing effort is concentrated on functionality where defects could create:

- Financial impact
- Failed payments
- Incorrect settlement
- Conflicting transaction states
- Significant customer or merchant disruption

## Evidence

QA conclusions should be supported by actual test results, reproducible defects, logs, API responses, screenshots, or transaction evidence where available.

## Final Principle

The purpose of QA is not simply to find bugs. It is to provide useful evidence about product quality, risk, and readiness for users.
