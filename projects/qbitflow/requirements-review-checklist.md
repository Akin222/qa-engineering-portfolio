# QBitFlow Requirements Review Checklist

## Functional Requirements

- [ ] Payment flow is clearly defined.
- [ ] Supported networks are identified.
- [ ] Supported assets are identified.
- [ ] Payment confirmation behavior is defined.
- [ ] Settlement behavior is defined.

## Business Rules

- [ ] Fee calculation is defined.
- [ ] Payment expiry is defined.
- [ ] Settlement amount is defined.
- [ ] Failure behavior is defined.

## Testability

- [ ] Requirements are specific enough to test.
- [ ] Expected outcomes are identifiable.
- [ ] Boundary conditions are understood.
- [ ] Error conditions are documented.

## QA Principle

Requirements should be reviewed for completeness and testability before execution begins.
