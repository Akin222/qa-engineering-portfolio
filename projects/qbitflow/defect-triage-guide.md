# QBitFlow Defect Triage Guide

## Objective

Provide a consistent approach for assessing defects discovered during payment-flow testing.

## Severity Considerations

### Critical

Potential financial loss, incorrect settlement, severe payment failure, or a major security issue.

### High

Important functionality is unavailable or a significant user journey is severely affected.

### Medium

A meaningful defect exists but a workaround or alternative path may be available.

### Low

Minor visual, usability, or non-blocking issue.

## Priority Considerations

Priority should consider:

- Customer impact
- Merchant impact
- Financial impact
- Frequency
- Reproducibility
- Release timing
- Availability of workarounds

## QA Principle

Severity describes the impact of a defect, while priority describes how urgently it should be addressed.
