# QBitFlow Stakeholder QA Summary

## Purpose

Provide stakeholders with a concise view of QA priorities and quality risks.

## What QA Evaluates

- Customer payment experience
- Payment processing
- Transaction confirmation
- Fee calculation
- Settlement
- Merchant visibility
- Error handling
- Integration behavior

## Stakeholder Questions

- Are critical payment paths functioning?
- Are known high-impact defects resolved?
- What remains untested?
- What risks remain?
- What evidence supports the current quality assessment?

## Communication Principle

QA reporting should communicate risk clearly without overstating certainty.

A product should not be described as "bug-free" simply because no defects were found during a particular test cycle.
